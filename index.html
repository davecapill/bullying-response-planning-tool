Bullying Response
Planning Tool

A structured way for school leaders and student support teams to consider the circumstances around bullying behaviour, identify proportionate responses and build a practical action plan.

Approximately 5–7 minutes to complete the initial planning questions

✓
Your case information stays with you

This tool does not upload or store your case information online. If you save a case, a file is downloaded to your device and stored wherever you choose to save it. If you enter identifiable student information, your school remains responsible for handling and storing the file securely.

Only enter information that is necessary for planning the response.

Professional judgement remains central

The tool does not determine whether bullying has occurred, prescribe disciplinary outcomes, replace policy or procedures, or provide clinical assessment.
