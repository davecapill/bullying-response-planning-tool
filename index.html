Bullying Response Planning Tool

The Bullying Response Planning Tool is an evidence-informed decision-support resource designed to assist school leaders and student support teams to systematically consider the circumstances surrounding bullying behaviour, identify relevant protective, preventative, teaching, response and recovery strategies, and develop a tailored action plan.

The tool supports professional judgement rather than replacing it, and is intended to help schools consider responses for the student experiencing bullying, the student engaging in bullying, peers, families and the broader school environment.

Important

This tool does not determine whether bullying has occurred, prescribe disciplinary outcomes, replace school policy or professional judgement, or provide clinical assessment.
